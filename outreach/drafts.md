## Biomch-L

Subject: GaitSPM — open-source graphical workflows for spm1d

GaitSPM provides a graphical workflow from prepared CSV/Excel gait curves to spm1d inference and vector figure export. It supports participant-aware import, paired and independent tests, and adjusted/factorial models with documented restrictions. Reproducible numerical comparisons are included. I welcome feedback on usability and reproducibility from biomechanics researchers.
